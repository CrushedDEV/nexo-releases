# Code of Conduct

Nexo's issue tracker is a place to report problems, share ideas and help each other. To keep it useful and welcoming for everyone, we ask all participants to follow these guidelines.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, even when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before jumping to conclusions.
- Keep discussions focused on Nexo and constructive: explain problems clearly and stay open to other points of view.
- Respect people's time: search before posting and keep reports concise.
- Protect privacy — yours and others'. Don't share personal data, credentials or private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to do so.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Spam, advertising, or deliberately derailing conversations.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a problem, open an issue (or, for sensitive matters, use the private contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled confidentially and as quickly as possible.

## Scope

This code applies to all of Nexo's public spaces, including this repository's issues and discussions, and to anyone representing the project.
